Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who is responsible

This website, thomasmoor.org, is operated by Thomas Moor Consulting (“I”, “me”), which is the data controller for the personal data described below.

For any question about your data, write to contact@thomasmoor.org.

Contact form

When you use the contact form, I receive the information you enter: your name and, if you provide them, your phone number, email address, the service you are interested in, your timeframe and your message.

This data is used only to answer your request and, if we work together, to prepare and manage our collaboration. The legal basis is the steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and my legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

Form messages are transmitted by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service that forwards them to my mailbox. I keep enquiries for up to three years after our last contact, unless a contract requires a longer retention period.

Analytics and cookies

With your consent only, this site uses Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd.) to measure audience anonymously: pages visited, approximate location, device type and how you arrived on the site. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.

Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<ID>, which last up to two years. They are not set if you decline. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page.

Your choice itself is stored in your browser (local storage entry tm_consent). It contains no personal data.

Hosting and third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may record technical data such as your IP address in server logs for security purposes. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Some of these providers are located outside the European Union. Transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email contact@thomasmoor.org.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority, for example the CNIL in France or the authority of your country of residence.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site or the services it uses change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
